Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and account password, as well as the expense data you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and device information, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to understand usage patterns and improve Spendly's features.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure needs such as hosting and email delivery, and these providers only process data as necessary to provide their service to us. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page. We're happy to help clarify how your information is collected, used, or stored.
